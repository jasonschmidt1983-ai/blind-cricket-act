SAME GAME. A DIFFERENT WAY TO PLAY.

Built on sound.
Powered by teamwork.

Blind cricket is cricket adapted for people who are blind or have low vision. An audible ball and clear communication bring the game to life.

It’s the familiar joy of a great shot, a shared celebration and being part of a team. You don’t need to have played before. We’ll help you find your feet.
