Player pathways

Your game can keep growing.

Try. Learn. Train. Compete. Represent. Explore a pathway that values development and what you contribute to a team.

One step at a time

Try → Learn → Train → Compete → Represent

  1. 01

    Try

    Ask about an introductory opportunity and discover the audible ball.

  2. 02

    Learn

    Build confidence with the basics, clear calls and team communication.

  3. 03

    Train

    Develop your skills through practice at a pace that works for you.

  4. 04

    Compete

    Ask about suitable competition and the requirements to take part.

  5. 05

    Represent

    Explore representative opportunities and the current selection process.

What contributes to selection?

The pathway values the following principles. Ask the club for the applicable eligibility requirements and current selection criteria.

  • Participation and engagement.
  • Skill development.
  • Performance.
  • Team contribution.

Opportunities depend on available competitions and selection. Progression is not automatic, and representative places are not guaranteed.

Progress at your own pace

Some players want a social connection and a regular activity. Others want to test their skills in competition. Both are valuable reasons to play; you do not have to pursue representative cricket to belong.

Representative opportunities

Ask the club about the current route into competitive and representative cricket. Eligibility, classification requirements, selection processes and available competitions should be confirmed before making plans.
