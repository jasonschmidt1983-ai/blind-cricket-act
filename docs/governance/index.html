Governance & policies

Trust starts with clear information.

Find public national integrity information and understand which club documents are still awaiting publication.

Club documents

Approved club governance documents, a constitution, a code of conduct and privacy or safeguarding policies have not been supplied for publication. They will be linked here when approved versions are available. This page is not a substitute for those documents.

Respect on and off the field

Respectful communication, listening to access needs and taking concerns seriously help make sport welcoming. These are participation principles, not a published club code, a legal waiver or a statement of disciplinary procedures.

National integrity information

Blind Sports Australia publishes information about the National Integrity Framework. Use the public source below to understand the framework and find current reporting guidance. The source explains the matters and processes it covers.
