Your first session, explained

A little preparation helps you focus on the game. Here are the questions worth asking before your first visit.

Confirm the essentials

Before leaving home, check the session date, start time, venue and meeting point with the club. Current session details are not published here, and arrangements can change. Ask how updates will be shared.

Ask what you need

Check what clothing and footwear are suitable, what equipment to bring and what is available at the session. If you are attending with a parent, carer or support person, confirm those arrangements too.

Get familiar with the space

Ask how the ground and the activity will be introduced. An agreed meeting point, verbal explanation or time to explore the layout may help. Confirm the support you need rather than assuming it will be available.

Give yourself room to learn

Your first visit is a starting point, not a test. Ask how the audible ball works, request an explanation when you need one and take time to become familiar with the basics. Being new is a good reason to ask questions.

Keep the first enquiry simple

You can ask ‘What is the next opportunity to try blind cricket?’ without sending medical or classification documents. If formal registration is needed later, ask for the appropriate secure process.
