Selected to represent the ACT

Our official team announcement brings together experience, talent and determination as preparations begin for Ballarat. We congratulate the selected athletes and thank everyone who participated in the selection process and continues to support the growth of blind cricket in the ACT.

Pride, determination and sportsmanship

The National Cricket Inclusion Championships give players the opportunity to represent their state or territory on the national stage. We wish our squad the very best as they prepare to represent the ACT with pride, determination and sportsmanship.
