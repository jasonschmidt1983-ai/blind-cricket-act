Report a safety issue

Find the right help for a safety concern.

If someone is in immediate danger in Australia, call 000. For other concerns, use the appropriate reporting channel—not a public social-media post.

Immediate danger

Call 000 for police, fire or ambulance in an emergency in Australia. If you can do so safely, move away from immediate danger. This website is not monitored as an emergency service.

Integrity and safeguarding concerns

Blind Sports Australia’s National Integrity Framework page provides public information and access to reporting guidance. Check that source for the appropriate channel and the types of concerns it handles. Do not submit a report through this website.

Club safety contact

A dedicated club safety contact has not yet been published here. For a general question about who the appropriate contact is, you may ask the club without describing the incident or identifying the people involved. Do not share allegations, medical information or details about children on Facebook.

Keep sensitive details in the right channel

Use an appropriate official reporting channel for the details of a concern. This website has no safety-reporting form and does not collect, store or investigate reports.
